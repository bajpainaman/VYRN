# Surface: VYRN landing (/)

Mode: Persuade. Code-led (no image generation on this machine).

- Audience: technical evaluators, trading firms, operators and engineers (PRODUCT.md). The first viewport must convince a sceptical HFT/systems reader.
- Actions: read the design (primary), open spec 00–11, go to GitHub, request a license, contact.
- Proof on hand: DESIGN.md §0 six lines, §1 comparison table, §9 week-one benchmarks (none run yet), §10 grilling. Every number keeps its kind.
- Feels wrong: crypto-launch energy, generic dev-tool SaaS, hiding the depth.
- Companion surface: /docs (Read mode) inherits this world on the white original ply.

## Direction contract

THESIS: VYRN is a paper order ticket that a time clock stamps, only at 1 µs. The page is the ticket. Its printed fields are the real 16-byte wire format. It refuses the centred headline over a metric strip and a feature grid.

OWN-WORLD: canary carbon ply as the ground, a pink duplicate ply, the white original ply for reading. Carbon-black ruled form cells with 2px rules. Buy blue and sell red are used only for side. Violet time-clock stamps carry every timestamp and status. Perforated edges. Faces: Archivo set condensed for display and expanded for labels, Martian Mono for fields and bytes, and Doto dot-matrix for stamps only.

STORY: the visitor sees one order land, get stamped and fill within budget, then reads the six-line answer. Each claim is a ledger row linked to its spec ID. The visitor ends on the design, the spec, GitHub or a license request.

FIRST VIEWPORT: one ticket at 85% of the viewport, with the pink copy offset behind it ("PLY 2 → DATA TIER"). The headline sits in the INSTRUCTIONS field. A live HOME panel shows TYO, LDN or NYC from UTC and the next handoff. A fields row shows TYPE SIDE FLAGS TICK QTY CLIENT_REF. A routing-box row closes the ticket, and its first box, "Read the design", is the inked primary action. A violet RX stamp crosses the instructions.

FORM: The Order Ticket, list position 4 of 7, seed:9132de8e8875fa42. Signature interaction: "Stamp an order" runs a labeled simulation on spec budgets. RX, CUT, FILLED Δ and FINAL n−f stamps land in sequence at fixed registration. Motion grammar: stamps strike with a short overshoot and carbon plies slide. Nothing else moves.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
